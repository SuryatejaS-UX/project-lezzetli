Heritage & Craft

Crafted With Whole Spices, Live Fire & Heart

Naveen's Kitchen Lezzetli was born from a passion for two of the world's most aromatic culinary cultures. On one side, the ancient tradition of grilling tender marinated meats over glowing charcoal embers. On the other, the royal heritage of authentic Hyderabadi Dum biryanis, aromatic curries, and time-honored South Indian kitchens.

Every morning in our Newbridge kitchen, whole spices—cardamom pods, cloves, cumin, and Kashmiri chilies—are freshly roasted and ground. Our naans are slapped by hand against the roaring clay walls of our tandoor oven, ensuring authentic blistered perfection on every plate.
